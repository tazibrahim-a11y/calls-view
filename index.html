How sourcing calls are scored

Each sourcing call is scored on 4 criteria. The weighted formula is: (Opening×2 + Qualification×3 + Objection×2 + Meeting×3) ÷ 50 × 5. Meeting is weighted highest because booking a next step is the only tangible output of a sourcing call.

Opening (w2) — Did the rep establish context, relevance, and the purpose of the call within the first 2 minutes?
Qualification (w3) — Did the rep confirm client type, size, industry, service model, and geography?
Objection (w2) — Were concerns handled confidently? Score 3 if no objection arose.
Meeting (w3) — Was a specific time confirmed by both sides? 5 = date locked, 3 = agreed in principle, 1 = no next step.
● ≥4.0 Strong ● 3.0–3.9 Developing ● <3.0 Needs review

How management calls are scored

Each management call is scored on 6 criteria. The weighted formula is: (Pipeline×3 + MAP×3 + Blocker×3 + Engagement×2 + Competitive×2 + Next step×2) ÷ 75 × 5. Pipeline, MAP, and Blocker are weighted highest — they directly determine whether a partnership is generating revenue.

Pipeline (w3) — Were specific clients discussed by name, with status and expected close timing?
MAP (w3) — Were MAP commitments reviewed — what was agreed vs what has actually happened?
Blocker (w3) — Was at least one blocker surfaced, root cause explored, and a resolution agreed?
Engagement (w2) — Is the relationship deepening? New contacts, joint activities, or increasing frequency.
Competitive (w2) — Were competitor signals explored? Score 3 if no competitor arose in the call.
Next step (w2) — Was a specific date and owner confirmed by both sides before the call ended?
● ≥4.0 Strong ● 3.0–3.9 Developing ● <3.0 Needs review

How discovery calls are scored

Discovery calls are the first substantive conversation with a potential partner. The goal is to qualify fit — confirming client base, service model, decision access, geography, and identifying the right champion. Scored on 5 criteria: Customer base & service model (w3) · Access to decision moment (w3) · DM/champion identified (w3) · Geography confirmed (w2) · Next step quality (w3).

How demo calls are scored

Demo calls validate a specific use case and move toward a signed partnership. The goal is a "yes, this works for us." Scored on 5 criteria: Use case clarity (w4) · Value recognition (w3) · Workflow fit (w3) · Commitment signal (w3) · Next step quality (w2). Use case clarity is weighted highest — if a concrete workflow, client type, and trigger moment aren't identified, the rest doesn't matter.
