How demo calls are scored — Formula: (Use case×4 + Value×3 + Workflow×3 + Commitment×3 + Next step×2) ÷ 75 × 5
Use case clarity (w4) — Specific workflow + client type + trigger moment validated from partner's words?
Value recognition (w3) — Partner explicitly confirmed relevance: "this is useful", "our clients need this"?
Workflow fit (w3) — Moss placed in existing process with friction level (Easy/Medium/Hard)?
Commitment signal (w3) — Partner willing to refer? Named clients, agreed process, or concrete action?
Next step quality (w2) — Concrete step toward formalising: MAP, agreement sent, enablement?

How management calls are scored — Formula: (Pipeline×3 + MAP×3 + Blocker×3 + Engagement×2 + Competitive×2 + Next step×2) ÷ 75 × 5
Pipeline (w3) — Specific clients by name, with status and expected close timing?
MAP (w3) — Commitments reviewed: agreed vs actually happened?
Blocker (w3) — One blocker surfaced, root cause explored, resolution agreed?
Engagement (w2) — Relationship deepening? New contacts, joint activities, increasing frequency?
Competitive (w2) — Competitor signals explored? Score 3 if none arose.
Next step (w2) — Specific date and owner confirmed by both sides?
